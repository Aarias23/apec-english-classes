ENGLISH 2 · FINAL WRITTEN PROJECT

Write it.
Defend it.

Your group will write one formal report in English and present its main ideas to the class. The report and oral defense must connect the content of Units 1–4 through one real-world mission.

60%written report
40%oral defense
1,400–1,800words per team
8–10oral minutes
TWO REQUIRED PARTS

One project, two products.

Part I: submit one coherent PDF in English.

Part II: explain and demonstrate the project without reading the report. Every member writes and speaks.

PART II · ORAL DEFENSE · 4 POINTS

Defend your project

The presentation summarizes and demonstrates the report; it does not reproduce every paragraph. Each member must own one role and speak.

8–10minutes per team

Plan approximately two minutes for each member and leave time for transitions.

4/4members speaking

Without a speaking role, the oral component is incomplete for that student.

5–8visual slides

Use keywords, maps, schedules and evidence. Do not place report paragraphs on slides.

1+audience question

The team must demonstrate that it understands and can defend its proposal.

Required sequence

Introduce the mission · explain the proposal · demonstrate a dialogue, interview or role-play · show evidence from Units 1–4 · conclude · answer questions.

FROM DRAFT TO DEFENSE

Four-stage workflow

At every checkpoint, the team shows both written progress and preparation for the oral defense.

  1. 1
    Plan and assign

    Study the mission, assign the four roles, answer the driving question and outline the report.

    Outline + role contract
  2. 2
    Draft and document

    Write the assigned sections and create the map, schedule, menu or supporting evidence.

    Complete first draft
  3. 3
    Revise and rehearse

    Edit as one team, check sources and language, create visual support and time the presentation.

    Final PDF + rehearsal
  4. 4
    Submit and defend

    Submit the PDF, present for 8–10 minutes and respond to at least one question.

    Final assessment
