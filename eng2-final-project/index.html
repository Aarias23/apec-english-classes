SAME RULES · DIFFERENT STORIES

The common brief

Every member must speak naturally and contribute visible evidence. Notes are allowed; reading a full script is not.

01

One connected story

Create a clear beginning, problem or purpose, solution, and closing. Include smooth transitions between speakers.

02

One visual product

Use 5–8 slides, a poster, a brochure, a map, or a physical display. Favor keywords and visuals over paragraphs.

03

Equal speaking

Each student speaks for approximately 1½–2 minutes and uses language from at least two units.

04

Live interaction

Include a dialogue, interview, demonstration, or role-play. Finish by answering one audience question in English.

Minimum language evidence
  • 4 simple-present sentences about routines or schedules
  • 4 food or shopping expressions, including quantities
  • 4 simple-past sentences and 3 direction expressions
  • 4 job words, 4 ability sentences, and 4 obligation sentences

FROM IDEA TO PERFORMANCE

Four-stage workflow

Use the checkpoints to divide the work early and avoid assembling four separate mini-presentations.

  1. 1
    Understand & assign

    Read the mission, choose roles, agree on one story and list the required language.

    Checkpoint 1
  2. 2
    Research & draft

    Create the map, schedule, menu, profiles, or other evidence your mission requires.

    Checkpoint 2
  3. 3
    Build & rehearse

    Prepare the visual, connect each part, time the performance and improve pronunciation.

    Checkpoint 3
  4. 4
    Present & respond

    Perform for 7–9 minutes, involve all four members and answer a question in English.

    Final day
