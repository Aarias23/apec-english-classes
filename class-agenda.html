Live Agenda

4-hour teaching sequence

0:00-0:10
10 min

1. Welcome + Academic Dates

Purpose: Start organized and make sure students know key dates.

Screen: Saturday Calendar

Teacher says: "Before we start Unit 1, let's look at the most important dates: midterm, final exam, and deadlines."

Students do: Identify midterm and final exam dates.

Product: Students know Aug 8 = midterm, Sep 12 = final.

0:10-0:25
15 min

2. Unit Opening + Can-Do Goals

Purpose: Show the learning route and final goal.

Screen: Teacher Slides 1-4

Teacher says: "Today grammar helps us do something real: make plans with other people."

Students do: Answer: "What are you doing this weekend?"

Product: One spoken sentence about the weekend.

0:25-0:50
25 min

3. Weather Vocabulary

Purpose: Build vocabulary students will use in plans.

Screen: Teacher Slides 6-7 Student Practice 2-3

Teacher says: "Weather changes the plan. Give me a weather word and an activity."

Students do: Listen, repeat, and create "It is..., so..." sentences.

Product: One weather + plan sentence.

0:50-1:15
25 min

4. Real English + Natural Reactions

Purpose: Make conversations sound more natural.

Screen: Teacher Slide 8 Student Practice 4

Teacher says: "These phrases help you react like a real speaker, not like a grammar robot."

Students do: Match phrases to situations and react to a partner.

Product: One mini exchange using a Real English phrase.

1:15-1:55
40 min

5. Grammar 1: Simple Present vs Present Progressive

Purpose: Contrast routines with actions happening now.

Screen: Teacher Slides 9-12 Student Practice 5

Teacher says: "Look for the time expression. It tells you if the action is a habit or happening now."

Students do: Complete grammar quiz and explain two answers.

Product: Two correct tense examples: one habit, one action now.

1:55-2:10
15 min

6. Break + Reset

Purpose: Give students a short break and prepare for speaking.

Screen: Keep agenda visible.

Teacher says: "When we return, we use the grammar to invite someone and make a real plan."

Students do: Break. Optional: write one plan for this week.

Product: Students return ready for speaking.

2:10-2:50
40 min

7. Grammar 2 + Invitations

Purpose: Use present progressive for future arrangements and invite a friend.

Screen: Teacher Slides 13-16 Student Practice 6

Teacher says: "The verb looks present, but the time expression makes it future."

Students do: Build and practice invitation dialogues.

Product: Pair dialogue with invitation, answer, time, and place.

2:50-3:20
30 min

8. Listening + Reading Connection

Purpose: Identify plan details in spoken/written context.

Screen: Teacher Slides 17-18 Student Practice 7

Teacher says: "When you hear a plan, catch four things: who, where, when, and what."

Students do: Answer plan-detail questions.

Product: Complete who/where/when/what answers.

3:20-3:40
20 min

9. Writing: Trip Blog Paragraph

Purpose: Transfer unit language to short writing.

Screen: Teacher Slides 19-20 Student Practice 8

Teacher says: "A blog tells information and feelings. Use adjectives to make it interesting."

Students do: Write a short blog paragraph with one adjective and one future plan.

Product: 3-5 sentence blog paragraph.

3:40-4:00
20 min

10. Final Role-Play + Exit Ticket

Purpose: Produce the final oral task and close with quick assessment.

Screen: Teacher Slides 21-26 Student Practice 9-10

Teacher says: "Your final plan needs activity, time, place, weather, reason, and a backup plan."

Students do: Present or rehearse group plan. Complete exit ticket.

Product: Short role-play/group explanation + exit ticket.
