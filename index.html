Unit 1: Making Plans

Students practice inviting friends, making arrangements, describing weather, talking about habits and actions now, and using the present progressive for future plans.

Speaking Weather vocabulary Present progressive Travel blog Collaboration

Class Goal

By the end of the session, students can make plans that work for everyone and describe future arrangements using natural spoken English.

Lesson Plan

4-Hour Sequence

0-15

Warm-up: Today and This Weekend

Students answer two prompts: "What do you usually do after class?" and "What are you doing this weekend?" Use the answers to preview the difference between routines and future arrangements.

Spanish support: "usually" indica rutina; "this weekend" puede indicar un plan futuro ya organizado.

15-45

Vocabulary: Weather and Plans

Teach and drill weather words. Students ask and answer: "What's the weather like today?" and "What's the weather like in...?" Then they connect weather to plans: "It's rainy, so I'm staying home."

45-85

Grammar Lab 1: Simple Present vs Present Progressive

Contrast habits with actions happening now. Students sort sentences, correct errors, and create personal examples.

English: "I play basketball on Fridays" = habit. "I'm playing basketball now" = action in progress.

85-100

Break

Optional pair challenge: write three true plans for this week.

100-145

Speaking: Invite a Friend

Model useful chunks: "Are you doing anything on Saturday?", "Would you like to...?", "Sorry, I can't. I'm..." Students build short invitation dialogues and perform them.

145-180

Grammar Lab 2: Present Progressive for Future Plans

Students describe arranged plans: "We're meeting outside school." "She's visiting her aunt." They complete a mini travel itinerary and ask follow-up questions.

180-220

Reading & Writing: Trip Blog

Students identify the introduction, useful adjectives, and future-plan sentences in a model blog. Then they draft a short blog about a weekend away or cultural event.

220-240

Global Skills + Exit Ticket

Groups plan an activity that works for everyone. Exit ticket: one weather word, one invitation phrase, one future arrangement, and one thing they can improve.

Grammar Lab

Choose the Best Form

Simple present = habits and routines. Present progressive = actions now or future arrangements when the context is future.

Speaking Practice

Invitation Role-Plays

Role-Play A

Student A: Invite your friend to go swimming on Saturday.

Student B: You cannot go. You are visiting family.

Role-Play B

Student A: Invite your friend to watch a basketball game.

Student B: Accept and arrange the time and place.

Role-Play C

Group: Plan a weekend activity everyone can enjoy.

Goal: Ask for opinions, explain ideas, and agree on one plan.

Useful Phrases

  • Are you doing anything on Saturday?
  • Would you like to go swimming?
  • Sorry, I can't. I'm visiting my grandparents.
  • That sounds great.
  • What a bummer! = Que pena.
  • You know what? = Sabes que?
  • Get out of here! = No puede ser.
  • Let's meet outside school.
