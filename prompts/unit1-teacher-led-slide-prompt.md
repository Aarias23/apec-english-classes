# Teacher-Led Slide Guide Prompt - Unit 1

Act as an instructional designer and English teacher.

I want to create a teacher-led lesson guide in slide format for the following topic:

**Unit 1 - Making Plans**

Student level:

**A2**

Communicative objective:

By the end of the lesson, students should be able to invite a friend, respond to invitations, make arrangements, describe weather conditions, and explain future plans using the present progressive.

Final expected product:

Students will perform a short role-play or group explanation where they plan a weekend activity, invite classmates, discuss the weather, agree on time and place, and explain why the plan works for everyone.

Reading materials or context:

The unit focuses on making plans, weather vocabulary, simple present vs present progressive, present progressive for future arrangements, invitations, travel plans, a short travel blog, and collaborative planning. Students learn useful expressions such as "Are you doing anything on Saturday?", "Would you like to...?", "Sorry, I can't. I'm...", "What a bummer!", and "You know what?" They also practice organizing ideas with reasons, examples, and opinions.

Design the content for a didactic slide sequence that can function as the teacher's main classroom screen.
The sequence must guide students through the full Unit 1 learning path, not only the final speaking task:

- unit overview and can-do goals
- weather vocabulary
- Real English phrases
- simple present for habits and routines
- present progressive for actions happening now
- contrast between simple present and present progressive
- present progressive for future arrangements
- time expressions
- invitations and responses
- making arrangements with time and place
- listening for travel plan details
- reading a travel blog
- writing a short blog paragraph with adjectives
- culture connection
- global skills: making plans that work for everyone
- connectors for logical thinking
- final oral production
- quick assessment / exit ticket

The sequence must guide students from understanding the unit content to producing an oral task in English.

Required structure:

1. Opening Frame
- Introduce the topic clearly and meaningfully.
- Include a short line the teacher can say.

2. Big Idea
- Explain the central idea of the topic.
- Include any essential concept students must understand.

3. Reading / Context Connection
- Summarize the most important ideas from the reading material.
- Do not copy long passages.
- Transform the reading into simple, teachable ideas.

4. Key Concepts
- Explain 2-4 important concepts.
- For each concept include:
  - simple definition in English
  - brief explanation
  - model sentence
  - student speaking frame

5. Problem Map / Idea Map
- Organize causes, effects, symptoms, characteristics, or main elements of the topic.

6. Language Focus
- Include key vocabulary.
- Include useful grammar structures.
- Include connectors for organizing ideas.

7. Cause and Effect / Logical Thinking
- Show how students can connect ideas using:
  - because
  - as a result
  - however
  - therefore
  - in my opinion
  - for example

8. Guided Practice
- Provide short questions for pairs or groups.
- Include speaking frames.

9. From Understanding to Production
- Explain how students move from understanding the topic to producing an oral response.
- Divide the process into steps:
  - define
  - explain
  - give an example
  - express an opinion
  - propose a solution or conclusion

10. Final Product Model
- Write a short example of the expected oral response.
- It must be clear, natural, and appropriate for A2 students.

11. Launch Activity
- End the guide by explaining what students will do next.
- Include clear instructions for the final speaking task.

Output format:

Organize the content as slides.
For each slide, use this format:

Slide title:
Purpose:
Main text:
Teacher says:
Student prompt:
Useful language:

Style:
- Clear, visual, and easy to project.
- Not too much text per slide.
- Focused on speaking.
- Student-centered.
- The teacher facilitates, but the sequence guides the students.
- Use English only.
